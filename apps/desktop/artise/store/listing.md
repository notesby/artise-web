# Artise in the Microsoft Store: listing texts

What to paste into Partner Center's Store listing, one block per language. The words follow the phones' Play listing
(artise-android `artise/store/listing/`), changed where the desktop app does something else. Spanish (Mexico) is
the package's first language (`appx.languages` in `src/artise/build/builderConfig.ts`).

- Product name: **Artise** (the package's display name).
- Privacy policy URL: https://artise.co/privacy
- Website: https://artise.co
- Support contact: https://artise.co/help
- Copyright: © 2026 Artise. Based on Element, by New Vector Ltd and Element Creations Ltd.
- Source code (AGPL): https://github.com/notesby/artise-web
- Category: Social.
- Screenshots: the welcome, the rail with two workspaces, a chat with Ari, Notes, a call. Take them from a beta
  build on Windows 11 with the test workspace (mas-k8s), in each language.

Chat privado y cifrado para tu familia o tu equipo, con Ari, un asistente que recuerda. Todos tus espacios de
trabajo en una ventana.

**Description**

Artise es un chat privado para tu familia o tu equipo, en tu propio servidor Artise. Viene con Ari, un asistente
que recuerda lo que te importa. En la computadora, todos tus espacios de trabajo quedan abiertos a la vez.

Privado desde el diseño
• Los chats están cifrados de extremo a extremo entre tus dispositivos.
• Tus mensajes viven en el servidor de tu espacio de trabajo, no en una gran plataforma.

Ari, el asistente de tu espacio

Notas compartidas en cada chat
• Cada chat tiene sus propias notas: recetas, viajes, proyectos, la wiki del equipo.

Todo lo que necesita un chat
• Llamadas de voz y video, y compartir tu pantalla.
• Fotos, videos, mensajes de voz y archivos; arrástralos a un chat.

Artise necesita una cuenta en un servidor Artise. Quien organiza tu espacio de trabajo te manda una invitación.

Artise está hecho sobre Element, el cliente de Matrix de código abierto, y también es código abierto bajo la

## en-US

**Short description**

Private, encrypted chat for your family or team, with Ari, an assistant who remembers. All your workspaces in one
window.

**Description**

Artise is a private chat for your family or team, on your own Artise server. It comes with Ari, an assistant who
remembers what matters to you. On your computer, all your workspaces stay open at once.

Private by design
• Chats are end-to-end encrypted between your devices.
• Your messages live on your workspace's own server, not in a big tech platform.
• No ads. Crash reports only if you say yes, and never your messages or notes.
• Search in encrypted chats happens on your computer.

All your workspaces, in one window
• Sign in once with your Artise ID (Apple, Google, Microsoft or email) and open your workspaces.
• Switch between them from the bar on the left, or with Ctrl+1…9.
• They all get messages at the same time, and each notification says which workspace it's from.
• Artise stays in the system tray and starts with Windows, so you don't miss anything.

Ari, your workspace's assistant
• Ask Ari in any chat it's part of: plans, reminders, lists, what was decided last week.
• Ari reminds you on time, right in the chat.

Shared notes for every chat
• Each chat has its own notes: recipes, trips, projects, the team wiki.

Everything a chat needs
• Voice and video calls, and screen sharing.
• Photos, videos, voice messages and files; drag them into a chat.

Artise needs an account on an Artise server. Your workspace's organizer sends you an invitation.

Artise is built on Element, the open-source Matrix client, and is itself open source under the AGPL:
https://github.com/notesby/artise-web

**Features**

- All your workspaces open at once
- End-to-end encrypted chats
- Ari, an assistant who remembers
- Shared notes in every chat
- Voice and video calls, and screen sharing
- Search in encrypted chats, on your computer

**Keywords**: chat, family, messaging, encrypted, assistant, notes, Matrix
